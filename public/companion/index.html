GETTING STARTED

✳

YOUR HOME BASE

Getting to know your dashboard

Your dashboard gives you a quick view of your connection and movement controls. It’s a simple place to see what’s ready and reach the tools you use most.

Your connection

When your computer is connected, you’ll see its status near the top of Home. If it isn’t connected, your settings and help options will be ready when it reconnects.

Quick access

Use the navigation at the bottom of your phone to open Settings, your Activity log, or Instructions. Tap Ask for help whenever you want to let your circle know you need them.

✦Take your time. Settings can be adjusted to what feels comfortable for you.
